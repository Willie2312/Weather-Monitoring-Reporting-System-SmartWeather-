Help

Quick answers to common questions about the Farmer Dashboard and how to use weather information for your farm.

Using the dashboard

The dashboard has three main areas:

  • Today — Current day’s weather: temperature, humidity, wind, and air pressure in one place.
  • Hourly forecast — Four time slots (e.g. 6 AM, 12 PM, 6 PM, 9 PM) so you can plan your day.
  • 7-day forecast — The week ahead (Today, Tomorrow, then day names) for planning work and planting.

Search & location

Search city: Type the name of your city or town and press Enter. The weather shown will update for that place.

Use my location: Click this to use your device’s location so you see weather for where you are. You may need to allow location access in your browser.

Weather is location-specific, so using the correct city or “Use my location” gives you the most accurate information for your farm.

Understanding today’s weather

In the Today card you see four values. Here’s what they mean and why they matter for farming:

  • Temperature — How hot or cold it is. Helps you decide when to work, irrigate, or protect crops from frost or heat.
  • Humidity — Amount of moisture in the air. High humidity can increase disease risk; low humidity can increase water stress. Useful for timing spraying and irrigation.
  • Wind — Wind speed and strength. Strong wind can damage plants, affect spraying, and increase evaporation. Use it to plan spraying, staking, and harvesting.
  • Air pressure — Often linked to rain or clear skies. Falling pressure can mean rain coming; stable pressure often means more settled weather.

Hourly forecast

The four hourly cards show expected weather at 6 AM, 12 PM, 6 PM, and 9 PM for today. Use them to:

  • Choose the best time of day for fieldwork (e.g. avoid midday heat or early morning wetness).
  • Plan spraying when wind is low and conditions are suitable.
  • Decide when to irrigate or when rain might reduce the need.
  • Prepare for temperature changes from morning to evening.

7-day forecast

The first card is Today, the second is Tomorrow, then the following days (e.g. Wed, Thu, Fri…). Use the week ahead to:

  • Plan planting or harvesting around dry or wet spells.
  • Schedule irrigation or drainage work.
  • Prepare for frost, heat, or storms.

Subscribe to alerts

Click Subscribe to alerts on the dashboard to sign up for weather notifications. You can enter a city or use “Use my current location” so alerts match your area. Alerts help you get important updates (e.g. heavy rain, strong wind, or frost) so you can act in time.

Farming advice

The Farming advice section shows tips and alerts based on current and forecast weather. Check it regularly for suggestions on irrigation, pest and disease risk, and best times for fieldwork. Advice is meant to support your decisions; always combine it with your own experience and local conditions.
