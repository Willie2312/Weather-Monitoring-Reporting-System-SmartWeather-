About Smart Weather

Bridging technology and agriculture through smart weather intelligence.

Overview

Smart Weather is an innovative web application designed to help farmers and citizens monitor weather conditions, receive instant alerts, and get personalized farming advice based on accurate weather data.

Our mission & vision

Our mission

To empower farmers with real-time weather insights and practical agricultural advice that enhances productivity and reduces losses.

Our vision

To build a connected and informed farming community through accessible and reliable weather technology.

Key features

  • Real-time weather updates: Accurate data from trusted weather APIs.
  • Farming advice: Crop-specific recommendations based on weather conditions.
  • Instant alerts: Notifications for rainfall, temperature changes and severe weather.
  • Admin analytics: Usage tracking and performance monitoring tools.

Why Smart Weather matters

Many farmers struggle to make informed decisions due to limited access to reliable weather information. Smart Weather solves this by providing timely and accurate data, helping users plan ahead and protect their livelihoods.
