Help

Common problems and solutions for general users. If your issue isn’t here, use Send Feedback from the dashboard menu.

Search for a city doesn’t work or shows no results

Problem: You type a city name but nothing happens or you see “No results”.

Solutions: Use the full, correct spelling of the city (e.g. “Freetown” not “free”). Try a nearby larger city if your town isn’t listed. Check your internet connection. Refresh the page and try again.

“Use my location” doesn’t work or shows wrong place

Problem: The app doesn’t find your location or shows weather for somewhere else.

Solutions: Allow location access when the browser asks. On phone, turn on Location/GPS for the browser. If it’s wrong, use Search city and type your city name instead. Avoid using location on VPN or strict privacy settings that block location.

Weather seems wrong or not updating

Problem: Temperature or conditions don’t match what you see outside, or the page doesn’t update.

Solutions: Refresh the page to get the latest data. Make sure the correct city is selected (check the location bar). Weather is based on the nearest weather station; small local differences can occur. If it’s still wrong, try another city nearby to compare.

I don’t get alerts or notifications

Problem: You subscribed to alerts but don’t receive any.

Solutions: Click Subscribe to alerts and ensure it shows Alerts on. Use Notification Signup from the menu (⋮) and enter a valid email. Check your spam folder. Make sure your browser or device allows notifications if you enabled them.

How to turn alerts on or off

Problem: You want to subscribe or unsubscribe from weather alerts.

Solutions: On the dashboard, use the Subscribe to alerts button. When it shows Alerts on, you’re subscribed. Click it again to turn alerts off. You can also set your city in the “Enter city or town” box and use “Use my current location” so alerts match your area.

I forgot my password or can’t sign in

Problem: You can’t log in or don’t remember your password.

Solutions: Use Change password from the dashboard menu (⋮) if you’re already logged in. If you’re on the login page, look for a “Forgot password?” link if available. Otherwise contact support (e.g. via Send Feedback) to reset your account.

Page looks broken or doesn’t fit my screen

Problem: Layout is messy, buttons are cut off, or text is too small on phone or tablet.

Solutions: Refresh the page and try rotating your device. Use the latest version of your browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Clear cache if the page still looks old or broken. If possible, try another device or browser to see if the issue is device-specific.

I want to send feedback or report a problem

Problem: You have a suggestion or something isn’t working.

Solutions: Open the menu (⋮) on the dashboard and click Send Feedback. Describe the problem or suggestion clearly; your email will open so you can send it to the team. We use this to fix issues and improve the app.
